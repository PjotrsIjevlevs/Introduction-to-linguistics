START WITH WHAT YOU ALREADY KNOW

Language feels effortless.

You can produce and understand sentences you have never heard before. Yet you rarely think consciously about all the patterns that make those sentences possible.

Exactly.

This gives linguists an important clue: language is productive and systematic rather than simply a list of memorized sentences.

03
A SYSTEM IN THE MIND

Your mental grammar.

A mental grammar is the system of knowledge that allows language users to understand and produce language.

Much of this knowledge is implicit: you can use it successfully without being able to consciously state the rules.

Language is more than writing.

Reading and writing are important for literate language users, but literacy is not required for grammatical competence. Human languages can be spoken or signed, and languages can have different writing systems — or none at all.

Phonotactics

Languages have constraints on how sounds can be combined. These patterns are part of what speakers know about their language, even when they cannot consciously state the rules.

06
PHONETICS ≠ PHONOLOGY

Same sounds. Different questions.

QUESTION A

Where is the tongue when a particular consonant is produced?

QUESTION B

Why can a particular sequence of sounds occur in one language but not another?

Remember

Phonetics asks what speech sounds are physically like.

Phonology asks how sounds are organized and patterned within a language.

CHECK YOUR INTUITION

In English, the p in pin is typically aspirated [pʰ], while the p in spin is typically unaspirated [p].

Are these necessarily two different phonemes?

08
ONE TINY FEATURE

[±voice]

Some consonants can be distinguished by whether the vocal folds vibrate during their production.

[+voice]

The vocal folds vibrate.

[z]
[-voice]

The vocal folds do not vibrate.

[s]
TRY IT PHYSICALLY

Put your fingers lightly on your throat.

Say ssssss. Then say zzzzzz.

Voice.

[z] is voiced, while [s] is voiceless. You can actually feel the difference as vibration in your vocal folds.
